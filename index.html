I'm Meet Bhorania, an AI Engineer and Full-Stack Developer based in London. I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Anglia Ruskin University and have since been building production-grade AI systems and web experiences for startups and growing businesses.

What sets me apart isn't just the tech — it's the ability to take a vague business problem, understand it deeply, and ship something that actually works. Whether that's a multi-agent AI pipeline, a RAG system, or a high-converting website, I bring the same obsession with quality to everything I build.

I've spoken at BSides Security London and GDG London I/O Extended, completed the Google DeepMind AI Research Foundations programme, and won recognition at the DEG Hackathon 2025 for GridFlex — a multi-agent platform for datacenter energy optimisation.

I'm currently open to the right opportunities — whether that's freelance projects, a founding team, or a full-time role where I can genuinely move the needle.

✨ Star Feature — Multi-Agent OS

OpsHive

Autonomous AI Operations Centre for Founders

OpsHive runs 6 specialised, concurrent agents — Sales, Finance, Marketing, Support, Ops, and a CEO Agent — communicating via Google's Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol and sharing system context via MCP. Once a founder enters their company description, agents auto-configure themselves, autonomously detect complex operational crises, collaborate in real time to build a resolution path, and surface only a single, critical decision to the founder.
